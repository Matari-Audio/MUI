Geometry & rendering
Execution: WebGL2 fragment shader, not Vello or Rust/WASM. Analytic rotated rounded rectangles; no per-frame CPU pixels or contour baking. One 352-byte parameter block plus a 6144-byte cached boundary buffer. Analytic line/arc intersections run only when geometry changes, not on a morph tick. This is the JavaScript/GLSL reference; native WGSL is not build-validated. GPU time is asynchronous and separate from CPU submission; no performance claim about your device. Arbitrary contours, masks, and shadows are not part of this fast path.
